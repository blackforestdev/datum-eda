checkpoint; it neither ratifies a mechanism nor executes native product proof.

## Independent Project Preferences specification review

A distinct reviewer checks the complete PPS-C01 through PPS-C03 inventory,
protected visuals and buildable contract before PPS-C04 owner disposition.
Check every field's real authority, typed query/mutation, seed/receipt relationship,
exclusions, menu/context behavior, input/focus/accessibility, failure/recovery,
undo/reopen and conformance obligations against current numbered decisions.
Account for every finding in the complete clause/disposition matrix.

Carry forward exact already-approved Units evidence without asking for duplicate
approval; verify its scope and current authority rather than copying old pending
wording as a fresh owner boundary. Accepted Units work does not approve other
categories. Conversely, PPS must not be reduced to the existing Units slice:
complete the required Project inventory and explicitly dispose every in-scope
category. Revision remains excluded unless a separate future owner decision
changes that boundary. Global machine authority, Project Working Units and
Publish/document conventions remain distinct.

This is future planning review, not Global Preferences development, a prototype
edit, migration execution, product acceptance or a new settings writer. Copied
producer evidence and self-review do not establish independence. Missing review
or mandatory unresolved semantics keep the owner packet incomplete.
