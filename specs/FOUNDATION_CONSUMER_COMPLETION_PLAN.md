## Scope and authority

This document repairs missing structured completion contracts for three existing
Frontier consumers. Their order, lifecycle, ownership, authorization and hard
prerequisites are unchanged. The Frontier selects their completion steps; this
document supplies requirements, not a rival selector. Recording a pending
execution step does not authorize it. No consumer is claimed by this repair.

PM001/002/019/023/026, the Universal Viewport Tooling specification, GUI
Write-Path Plan, and schematic/PCB authoring contracts remain product authority.
PM025 and the project-state policy govern transitions; PM041 governs delivery
evidence, enrollment and owner-selected trust. Nothing here adds a product
mechanism, dependency, prototype change or enforcement enrollment. Current
enforcement of the pilot alone must not be described as coverage of these lanes.

Before execution, each preflight must reconcile its complete evidence routes,
map requirements to observable behavior and failure cases, identify file owners,
and attach a reviewable delivery contract with exact fixture, environment,
native-input and reference provenance. Research resolves genuine unknowns;
already-ratified behavior is not reopened. Unresolved required behavior remains
blocking. Product choices and prerequisite changes require their applicable
owner/numbered-decision transaction, not this scheduling repair.

Each lane reserves an independent reviewer who did not implement the candidate.
Its owner-authorization packet must identify how that review will occur; an
unavailable reviewer is not replaced by implementer self-approval. Mandatory
native proof includes actual input and observable state transitions, not only
engine calls or screenshots. Test failures and defects carry explicit
dispositions. Acceptance must identify the exact reviewed candidate and evidence;
an earlier acceptance or general instruction to proceed cannot be reused.
