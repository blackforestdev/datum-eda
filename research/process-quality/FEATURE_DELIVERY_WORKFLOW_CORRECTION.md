Recorded 2026-10-08. This is evidence for PM059, not a rival roadmap or a new
product feature specification.

The owner reported that recurring documentation, testing, chores and regression
repair were crowding out new usable functionality. The preferred rhythm is build,
test, fix, document. The owner subsequently requested a management agent to
facilitate routine dispositions because manual approval relays defeat the purpose
of the established research and specifications, then explicitly required this
correction in governing documentation rather than another suggested prompt.

A read-only inspection of the last 200 commit subjects ending at `fc116eac` counted
47 feat, 50 fix, 67 docs, 24 chore and 12 test subjects. Path inspection counted
89 commits touching only documentation/governance, 103 touching non-test crate
source, and 90 touching test/fixture paths; these latter groups overlap. These
counts describe repository activity, not effort, elapsed time or feature value.
They justify reviewing the workflow; they do not establish a numerical quota.

Concrete process failures included repeated prompts to continue feature work
while new mechanisms remained unauthorized, serial owner copy/paste of review
receipts, and stopping after small administrative units. Necessary repair and
independent review retain their value; the correction removes repeated routing
through the owner and makes complete designer behavior the delivery unit.

PM059 supplies bounded delegated management authority, retains owner-reserved
product changes and independent acceptance evidence, and reconciles PM025, the
project-state policy and agent instructions. No existing acceptance obligation,
visual file lane, dependency restriction or active implementation scope is waived.

The owner subsequently requested a usable application whose real usage supplies
test feedback, then instructed this session to make that direction effective.

roadmap inspection still selected S5A native selection qualification, while GUI

priority gap; selection proof alone does not deliver a design-editing workflow.

qualification and schedules USER-EDIT-DELIVERY for the first actual board edit.
The existing S5A implementation, active ownership and unperformed acceptance
remain preserved; no fixture, review receipt or priority change proves editing.
