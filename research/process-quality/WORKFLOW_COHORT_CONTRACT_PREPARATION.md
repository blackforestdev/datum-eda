# Native cohort contract preparation basis

WORKFLOW-DELIVERY-IMPLEMENTATION / WDQ-I02;
`dat-wdq-rollout-implementation-ffy`. Route: `workflow-delivery-cohort-preparation`.

This local preparation carries forward the approved rollout implementation
scope and the complete foundation consumer completion plans. It is not external
research, product specification authorship, native proof or owner acceptance.

## Preparation boundary

The three machine contracts reserve full cohort scenario groups and five-phase
mappings. They deliberately have unresolved C01 questions and null production
handlers. Dispatch labels prefixed `pending.` are proposal identities, not real
runtime dispatch keys. C01 must replace them with reviewed production identities.
No fixture, event, executable, environment, independent reviewer or owner receipt
is invented. Structure validation is permitted; readiness must refuse.

The approved scenario groups are coverage containers, not single passing examples.
Each C01 owner must expand its groups into the complete domain/consumer/input
matrix, exact fixtures and per-scenario dimension dispositions before readiness.
Required dimensions below reserve obligations, not measured or implemented facts.
All original domain scope remains mandatory unless the owner expressly changes it.

## Foundations and input closure

Each cohort must resolve all eight foundation concerns against its full product
authority. The drafts conservatively reserve them as required for C01 review;
they do not grant a not-applicable waiver or claim an irrelevant capability.
For read-only selection, cancellation, undo and reopen obligations include proving
absence of authored changes; they do not introduce selection journal operations.
Likewise numeric/grid review must preserve the read-only boundary, not add editing.

The conservative candidate roots are `.cargo`, Cargo manifests/lock, all crates,
icon/menu sources and guarded Cargo policy/runner. Exact native fixtures and
producer dependencies are unresolved and must be added by C01; these roots are
not a complete approved build closure. Existing relevant Preferences source must
not be excluded just to avoid reproof. Production owners approve any narrowing.

## Product authority and acceptance

S5A remains complete read-only board/schematic selection, lifecycle, pane and
compound inspection. GUI write-path retains all P0/W1/W2/W3 dispatch and durable
transaction requirements, not merely one rename. Native authoring retains both
complete schematic and PCB domain contracts, not a doorway or one-edit substitute.
Their C02 execution authorizations, existing prerequisites, independent review
and exact owner acceptance remain separate and unchanged.

Full product routes, foundation dispositions, normal enabled consumers, exact
fixture/input closure, unresolved mechanisms, scope matrix and reviewer provision
must be resolved by the actual C01 owners. Accepted earlier evidence is carried
forward only with exact scope and current authority. No prototype is edited here.

This preparation route owns only this source and the three draft contracts.
Progress, enrollment proposals, future proof/review and acceptance receipts stay
outside their authority closure so writing evidence cannot change its own basis.
The drafts are not attached to the live Frontier or promoted into installed trust.
