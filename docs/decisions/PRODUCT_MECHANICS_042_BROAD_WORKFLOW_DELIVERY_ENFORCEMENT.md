## Owner-pinned coverage and transaction permissions

Policy schema 2 retains the schema-1 fields `decision_ref`, `enrolled` and
`legacy_baseline` and adds a closed `coverage` object with exactly:
`baseline_ref`, `rows`, `source_scopes`, `production_roots`, `new_item_rule`.
The rule is the literal `classification_required`. Commit references are full
resolving IDs, never a moving branch or an implicit HEAD trust fallback.

Each row has exactly `frontier_key`, `issue_id`, `category`, `boundary_ref`
and `external_handoff_ref`. Every Frontier key appears exactly once; its issue
identity agrees with the Frontier. Shared historical epic issue IDs do not make
Frontier keys interchangeable. Existing `{path, marker}` references resolve
uniquely in governed documents. Boundary files are pinned in their entirety.
Candidate policy changes require owner promotion, not a digest refresh.

Categories:

- `historical`: landed at the pinned baseline, with unchanged completion
  obligations and landing evidence. Live unblocks bookkeeping may change, but
  reopening or new execution requires reclassification. No retrospective native
  certification follows from this category.
- `specification`: an explicit non-execution completion plan, governed clause
  inventory and route-reviewed outputs. Implementation requires a separately
  classified successor; an authored document cannot ratify its own decisions.
- `product` and `infrastructure`: a matching-category delivery declaration is
  required before completed preflight or execution. Actual authority/readiness
  checks apply even before enrollment. Execution requires owner-promoted
  enrollment, not a candidate-only declaration.
- `deferred`: preserve the promoted lifecycle, no authorization and no claim.
  This includes the approved MCAD planned/none placeholder; classification does
  not force a lifecycle rewrite. Reactivation requires reviewed reclassification.
- `external_lane`: a non-null coordinated handoff reference and exact preserved
  authorization, governing documents, prerequisites, completion scope and selected
  step boundary. This is not a session-name exemption. Other steps and selected
  requirement/action authority cannot be rewritten under the exception.

Only external rows carry `external_handoff_ref`; others use null. No row stores
a second status, owner, assignee, lease or next-step selection.

Each source scope has exactly `frontier_key`, `step_ids`, `paths` and
`boundary_ref`. It refers to existing execution steps and normalized literal
paths within reviewed production roots. Every changed production path needs a
matching promoted scope, current execution authorization, selected in-progress
step and synchronized unexpired PM025 claim. Product/infrastructure also require
their promoted contracts. An external scope never excuses stale proof for
another enrolled consumer affected by that edit.

Ordinary staged checks compare the captured index with its HEAD parent; worktree
diagnostics include untracked and ignored inputs within the reviewed roots.
Exact candidate checks require ancestry from the explicit base and cover all
intervening source mutations, including reverted/deleted inputs and merge-parent
changes. Current permissions apply to that path union. This does not authenticate
past authors or infer expired claims from timestamps, and a clean new transaction
does not certify earlier bypassed commits.

## Specification completion and unresolved choices

Use the existing specification boundary reference for a governed, owner-pinned
JSON inventory. Its closed schema is `schema_version: 1`, `marker`,
`frontier_key`, `clauses`. Each clause has `id`, `step_id`,
`requirement_ref`, `authority_refs`, `requires_owner_decision` (boolean).
IDs are unique, all references map existing step requirements, and every step
requirement has at least one clause. Reviewers must assess actual normative
granularity; structural accounting cannot discover an omitted prose requirement.

A completed step references one governed JSON matrix with marker
`WDQ-CLAUSE-MATRIX.<Frontier-key>.<step-id>`. Its closed fields are
`schema_version: 1`, `marker`, `frontier_key`, `step_id`, `dispositions`.
Each disposition has `clause_id`, `disposition`, `evidence_refs`,
`owner_step_id`. Exactly the completed step's inventory clauses are covered,
with no duplicate or unknown ID. Inventory, output, authority and disposition
references resolve through complete fresh owning routes.

`documented` references an output and has null owner_step_id; it cannot resolve
a clause requiring an owner mechanism decision. `pending_owner` references the
open choice and names a pending owner-decision step transitively dependent on the
authored step; it cannot stand for a landed outcome or a completed decision.
`ratified` has null owner_step_id and references unchanged numbered controlling
doctrine in owner-promoted authority. Candidate-only doctrine is insufficient.
Completed owner steps and their referenced evidence must match promoted authority.
Pending authorship needs its reviewed inventory, not invented future outputs.

## Distinct review, enabled delivery and owner acceptance

A new delivery mapping has exactly `schema_version: 2`, `contract_path` and
`checkpoints`. Checkpoints are `ready`, `activate`, `verify`, `review`,
`accept`, in that dependency order. Non-null steps are distinct. Ready is
planning/governance; activate, verify and review are execution; product accept
is owner_decision. Infrastructure activate/accept remain null; review is required.
Legacy fieldless mappings retain their original shape and behavior.

For version-2 product delivery, at least one scenario must require the normal
dimension. Every consumer in such a scenario needs its real handler reference
at readiness and observed eligible/enabled invocation on every reviewed entry
surface in both producer proof and independent replay. An unavailable-only run
cannot establish required normal behavior. Explicit refusal/future-control
scenarios may mark normal not applicable with governed justification; they do
not replace the required enabled scenarios. Infrastructure cannot claim native
product delivery from its own successful checks.

Review completion validates an approved independent review, session exclusions,
the exact proof packet, matching fixture/inputs/executable/build/environment,
fresh event identities, correlations and accounting for all proof/replay defects
against known tracker issues. Findings may await owner disposition. Review does
not demand, fabricate or imply an ACCEPT receipt.

Acceptance additionally retains all PM041 trusted defect dispositions: blocking
defects closed with resolution bound to the exact replay, explicit nonblocking
deferrals, and the exact externally promoted owner receipt. A version-2 acceptance
transition cannot skip a reviewed baseline. Infrastructure review remains review,
not product acceptance. Independent replay and owner intent judgment are not
replaced by syntactic completeness or matching hashes.

## Exact promotion, storage and evidence of adoption

Promote only an independently reviewed complete candidate with exact base,
authority, environment, coverage, source scopes, contracts and owner disposition.
Preparation is non-operative. Stale base, dirty index/worktree, missing runner,
ambiguous mode or incomplete evidence must fail visibly before publication.
Partial activation must be reported as partial; repair is explicit and forward,
not an automatic destructive reset or silent hook bypass.

Use the resolved Git-common `.git/datum-wdq` support store for proposals,
immutable trusted runners and logs. Keep any currently referenced external
support until its replacement is installed and verified. Remove only positively
unreferenced owned artifacts; no broad sweep of Documents or shared build output.

The exact packet must also reconcile self-enrollment against actual completion
step kinds. The current I01-I06 plan has no planning/governance readiness step;
its names alone are not a valid mapping. Prepare and review an explicit
completion-plan adjustment with genuine readiness evidence. Do not relabel a
completed execution step or substitute R03 implementation authorization for a
delivery contract. This unresolved mapping prevents a claim that the rollout
infrastructure contract is ready or enrolled.

Product C01 owners retain actual handler, fixture, domain scope and prerequisite
review. Cohort mappings and scenario identities from the approved R03 packet are
not native evidence. Preferences remains with its existing session until an exact
coordinated migration or owner-approved bounded exception is recorded.

Full rollout acceptance requires installed main-checkout/hook/selector evidence,
independent gate review, explicit external-lane disposition, and three genuine
adoption cohorts with evidence-linked gap, refusal, rework, handoff, owner
correction and unresolved-defect measurements. Do not invent a baseline or count
future task names, hermetic fixtures or this amendment as completed adoption.
