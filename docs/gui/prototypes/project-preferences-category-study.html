Ordering rationale. Identity first, then the two things that govern how every number and drawing reads (Units, Drafting Standard), then the policy layer (Revision), then what the project enforces and consumes (Rules, Library), then what it emits (Output, Publish), then who may act (Team). Schematic and Board sit last and dimmed because their project-scoped content is thin today — one schematic descriptor exists, and the 13 pcb descriptors are machine Presentation, not project policy.
Nine high-confidence, two provisional. The dimmed pair are drawn so their placement is settled now rather than argued later, but neither has enough project-owned content to justify a populated pane yet.
