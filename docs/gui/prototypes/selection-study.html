Datum Selection Study S5-C09 evidence · pending owner review

Fixed-size reference for the ratified S5 selection visual contract (UVT §2.2.13/§2.2.16/§4.4, Rendering Book §2, OPEN-1…14 resolutions). Every panel illustrates ratified construction; exact color tokens remain governed by the Rendering Book.

1 · Construction — lift + internal glow + 2 px crisp object cue

Selection brightens the complete owned presentation (lift), adds the #CE5A92 internal soft glow, and a crisp object-shaped 2-physical-px screen-space cue following the actual silhouette — never a bounding box. Hover is a lighter 1.5 px transient; selection wins.

2 · Compound — equal members, focus in Inspector only

Every visible member of a compound receives the same full treatment. The optional focus member has no stronger canvas styling — it is identified in the Inspector inventory only; removing it promotes no one.

3 · Locked — greyed, fully selected, anchor padlock (glyph candidate)

Locked objects are slightly neutral-greyed, keep the complete normal selection treatment, suppress handles, and show a small anchor padlock only while selected/hovered. Glyph below is the governed candidate (icon_set id selection-lock-anchor) — HUMAN review required before use.

4 · Maximal collision — every channel survives

Compositing order: authored base → proposal ghost/dual-stroke → selection cue → topmost semantic diagnostic marker. Selecting a proposal or finding adds selection without erasing uncommitted or severity identity; no channel is recoloured into selection magenta (OPEN-12/OPEN-14).

5 · Active / inactive panes — identical projection, frame-only authority

An actual selection projects at identical full strength in every resolving pane, active or not. Pane frame/header/tool enablement alone communicate GUI mutation authority.

6 · Global Net & Bus — semantic subjects

Triple-click Global Net: one semantic subject whose complete visible resolved projection glows across schematic and PCB (junction dots included, OPEN-7); parent bodies stay merely related — exact authored baseline. Bus: spine + name + entries glow as one subject; scalar member nets never glow via membership (OPEN-8).

7 · Text & point objects — glyphs and semantic cores

Standalone text uses glyph selection with no persistent layout rectangle (handles only in the edit tool). Vias, junctions, and no-connects preserve their semantic cores and stay mutually distinguishable while selected; sub-2 px projections collapse to one minimum screen-space cue.

8 · Dense fallback — exact union mask, never partial

Beyond 65,536 detailed overlay primitives per pane/frame the entire pane switches atomically to an exact visible-silhouette union mask — never truncation, never a bbox. One budget wherever the cue draws (OPEN-14).

9 · Accessibility & zoom invariance

High contrast substitutes crisp rings/outlines for glow; distinction is never colour-alone (shape and pattern carry it — CVD-safe); selection never pulses (reduced motion: dashed ants stop animating, remain dashed). The crisp cue measures 2 physical px at every zoom.
