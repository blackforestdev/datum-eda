One window, recursive panes, Zoom and Full-Screen Stage are inherited from PM019 / PM021 unchanged; no preset, detached window or global editor mode is added. In every arrangement the focused pane carries the accent frame and the word FOCUS with a square glyph, the Console sits lower-left of the focused pane only, the single Inspector follows focus, and document / source / occurrence stay reachable. How the Navigator and Inspector are reached in a narrow window, and where refusal feedback sits, are candidate presentations. These are static frames: keyboard, assistive-technology and reduced-motion qualification need the ordinary application.
