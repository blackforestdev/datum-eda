S-QPCB connectivity revision review — an authored pin/net reassignment read against the existing board counterpart ○ TARGET · not delivered◇ SYNTHETIC

Candidate presentation for reviewing an explicit pin/net reassignment already authored in the root schematic on an existing mapped component, between two existing stable board-correspondent nets, against the board counterpart that an earlier transfer created. Native proof is PENDING — nothing in this family claims a delivered surface, runtime behaviour or a product PASS. Entry is the same retained root-schematic transfer review as frame P1: its existing card carries the trigger (Q1), and pressing it closes the card and opens the existing shared inline Proposal review in the same captured root context — no pane switch, no new window, tool, controller or gesture, and no new board-side entrypoint. The review shows the exact old and new terminal → pad → logical-net → board-net identities, the component's Part and Footprint as read-only data, and every affected Track, Via and Zone source ID; names support the stable identities and never replace them. Opening the review inspects the design source to prepare the immutable old/new diff; nothing changes before explicit acceptance. Applying changes only the qualified pad ↔ net correspondence, in one canonical transaction: package, pad and source IDs, placement, the pinned physical choice and all authored copper — its geometry and its old net assignments — are retained. There is no automatic reroute, no copper deletion or reassignment, no remap, no library adoption, no general ECO and no hierarchy support. Resulting shorts, unrouted connections and stale fill surface through the existing authorities — foreign-net contacts stay short diagnostics (PM054 C04) and fill currency is PM054 C05 — and require explicit ordinary PCB rework: reroute, refill, check. This review never claims clean or synchronized routing after Apply. Because current zone contact and fill authority require the full resolved model revision, the schematic rewire has already made affected zone fill stale before this review opens — zones are listed conservatively by retained source ID, as potentially affected, never as known current contact. While correspondence is pending or mismatched, partial local inspection is permitted but a complete Global Net is never presented (PM053 C01 stable Net identity and atomic transitions, C02 binding and incomplete design states — existing policy; no new rules or controls); a complete review — including its disclosed source-bound unknowns — is never a claim of complete physical connectivity, current fill or a complete Global Net (PM054 C04/C05 remain the authority). Net birth or retirement, ambiguous split or merge, a changed physical or library map, and unsupported scope or contact kinds are outside this bounded review and refuse here without design writes (Q7) — each remains available as its own ordinary deliberate flow; nothing in this family forbids normal source authoring. Tab applies only while the review is complete, current and ready; Esc always dismisses. Every part, net, name and ID drawn is an illustrative synthetic first-party example.
